# Product Guidelines - ComfyUI Story Studio

## Tone and Voice
Our communication style is **Supportive and Instructive**. We prioritize clear, step-by-step guidance to help users navigate the integration between the Story Studio and ComfyUI. We aim to be helpful companions in the creative process, demystifying complex technical steps without oversimplifying the underlying power of the tools.

## Visual and UI Principles
- **Functional Minimalism:** We believe in a clean, uncluttered interface. By prioritizing story data and essential action buttons, we reduce cognitive load and allow the user's creative content to remain the focus.
- **Information Density (Strategic):** While we value minimalism, we also recognize the needs of power users. We provide high-value data points and controls in a compact layout to minimize unnecessary navigation and context switching.

## Documentation and Knowledge Sharing
- **Clarity over Complexity:** When documenting internal logic or system interactions, we use simple language and clear diagrams. Our goal is to make the system's architecture and the relationship between Studio and ComfyUI easily understandable for both users and developers.

## Naming and Terminology
- **Balanced Approach:** We use a hybrid naming convention. Narrative-centric terms (like "Scenes" or "Stories") are used for high-level management to align with the user's creative goals. Technical terms familiar to the ComfyUI ecosystem (like "Checkpoints" or "Prompts") are retained for generative settings to ensure functional clarity and consistency with the underlying engine.

## Feedback and Error Handling
- **Action-Oriented Feedback:** When issues arise, we provide brief, clear descriptions of the problem followed immediately by a suggested next step. Our goal is to keep the user moving forward and minimize downtime by providing actionable paths to resolution.
